The App Master's First Day

Three App Master personas, one per real project, switched on at midnight and left to decide for themselves. Ten build-and-observe cycles later, this is what they did, what they could not do, and why.

THE QUESTIONCan an App Master drive a project autonomously this way?

Yes, up to the one act the mandate reserves for a person. Each App Master woke on its own schedule, read its six responsibilities and the project's state, decided which to run and how many, dispatched workers on Opus into isolated branches, filed what it found back into Personas, remembered coverage between wakes, and asked the operator for the decision it could not take.

The ceiling is the human accept. By 06:41 UTC every persona had swept its unchanged code, declared KPIs for its uncovered contexts and delivered every accepted idea it had. Seventy new backlog items sat pending. Ascent's seventh wake dispatched nothing and slept two hours, and said exactly why. From then on the loop's throughput was bounded by triage, which is the operator's, not the Director's, to open.

THE JOURNEYTen cycles, each built from the last one's evidence

Every cycle followed the same shape: observe the live loop, read the ledger and the workers' transcripts, name the one thing the evidence said was wrong, build it in an isolated worktree with its tests, merge between wakes, observe again. The order below is the order it happened.

CYCLE 0 · 23:00 to 00:30 UTC · commits 2a483866d, 535fc1552, bf54ddc0c, 3814f20d3

Build the loop

Found
App Master was already a runtime role in Rust, not a template. The living-agent attention loop was all admission and no decision: one lane per tick by fixed precedence, one dispatch per tick, least-recently-advanced pick, no priority anywhere, and responsibility_id never read on the trigger path. The 109 recipe rows in the live database still carried their June payloads; the v3 corpus had only ever reached fresh installs.
Built
The corpus as a starting line (every recipe draft, unversioned) with two new recipes: accepted-idea delivery and project KPI stewardship. A headless adoption door on the loopback bridge. Charter priority and pacing. A decision lane that asks the persona's model for a bounded plan and dispatches to capacity, code work into isolated worktrees. A seeder that upgrades stale builtin payloads.
CYCLE 1 · 00:27 to 02:45 UTC · commit 85e0386dc

First decisions

Measured
The first wakes went to the daily self-review and a memory pass; the loop idled at 900 seconds between ticks; two wake requests stayed queued because a wake was only consumed when the interval floor refused, which a fresh persona never hits.
Changed
A wake means decide. The loop never idles while App Masters are on. Then CandiDate decided on Opus with real reasons ("280 of 285 contexts carry no active KPI", "oldest of 5 taskless ideas") and both workers delivered: one re-validated the idea, found its premise half stale, and committed the part that still applied.
CYCLE 2 · merged 02:43 UTC · commit 58dc4c06d

Write-back

Measured
The workers wrote nothing back into Personas: the delivered idea stayed accepted with no task row, the steward's KPI existed only as code, findings were prose. A fleet session had no route and no working MCP: the sidecar was launched with the wrong token variable, and the protocol text parser had no propose_backlog key.
Changed
Four bridge routes (file an idea, report an outcome, declare a KPI, record a measurement), a task row minted at dispatch, a write-back block in every worker's brief, in-flight work and last-dispatch outcomes in the decision prompt, a boot sweep for orphaned ledger rows, the MCP token, the parser key. Thirty-three items landed in the next wave, including a critique of the loop filed by a persona about itself.
CYCLE 3 · merged 05:34 UTC · commit c8f9a7da3

Self-pacing

Measured
Every wake was spaced by a fixed 30-minute floor regardless of what the persona had in flight.
Changed
The plan names its next wake (10 to 240 minutes, clamped); admission honours it for App Masters only. personas-web chose 15 then 12 minutes and its notes read like an operator's: "4th try, 3 prior deaths, brief: find WHY KPI declares don't persist". The fifth run fixed it.
CYCLE 4 · merged 06:44 UTC · commit 5c841775d

The ceiling

Measured
Fleet workers rode the account's default model and three ended on the operator's own subscription limit, filed as finished work. And the finding that bounds the arc: with 60 pending and 0 accepted ideas, every persona had run out of permitted work.
Changed
The fleet path pins the charter's model. A limit or a FLEET:BLOCKED end reads as failed. A dispatch whose worker died without writing back hands its idea back. The daily cap counts charter dispatches, not decision rows.
CYCLE 5 · merged 08:46 UTC · commit 4be3e1604

Asking back

Measured
A persona told its worker to triage the backlog itself; the worker correctly refused ("no idea accept verb in the bridge; needs in-app triage") and was parked awaiting an answer nobody would give.
Changed
The plan can carry up to three asks; each becomes a review in Approvals with the listed ideas and the actions to take, accepting applies the verdicts and wakes the persona. personas-web raised one within an hour of the merge. On the way, a pre-existing UI defect: every pending review had been rendering its title with no reasoning under it.
CYCLE 6 · merged 09:0x UTC · commit b73d89989

Shipping at rung 2

Measured
Three delivery workers produced good commits and none pushed or opened a pull request: the Overnight engine's guardrail in their prompt forbade it while the mandate at rung 2 permits exactly that.
Changed
A rung-aware brief: at rung 2 the worker may push its branch and open the pull request with the operator's authenticated gh, never merge, never touch the default branch or CI. No pull request had appeared by the end of cycle 9; the first delivery under the new brief is still owed.
CYCLE 7 · merged 09:5x UTC · commit c3e9c2151

Parked workers

Measured
The parked worker held one of personas-web's two slots for over an hour.
Changed
App Master runs are unattended runs: swept after 15 minutes awaiting input, the worker's own blocked line kept as the reason. Verified rather than assumed: fleet workers had never occupied the persona's concurrency slots at all.
CYCLE 8 · merged 11:0x UTC · commit 3ee4ef6ee

Recipes learn

Measured
The scan recipes swept the same unchanged code repeatedly; the personas learned to defer them on their own. Delivery worked best of all six.
Changed
Scans read their last sweep and stop on unchanged code; findings are filed once, above the project's own bar; delivery confirms its previous item and writes every outcome back; KPIs are declared before any probe. Six recipes, all still draft, upgraded in the live rows by the seeder.
CYCLE 9 · merged 11:0x UTC · commit c34b74c22

Capacity, honestly

Measured
"Two parallel sessions per project" had been enforced by the persona's own reading of its ledger. And at 10:17 an unattended review policy auto-approved the operator ask as routine severity and applied the accept; a machine took the decision the mechanism exists to reserve for a person.
Changed
Active fleet workers count against max_concurrent, with the breakdown in the prompt. Any review carrying source = app_master_ask is excluded from auto-triage at every severity.

DECISIONSFor the operator

These are the things the Director measured and deliberately did not decide.

  • Three asks are waiting in Approvals right now. pending Ascent asks you to accept a batch of its 27 pending ideas and to clear seven duplicate "Document ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL" tasks stuck in flight since August 26; CandiDate asks how to handle the structural accepted idea a94f3c09 that keeps blocking its delivery loop. Each carries the listed items and the actions to take.
  • Open the accept door, or keep it human. ceiling Eighty items wait. The product already has Action::BacklogTriage under a project's full autopilot with mechanical triage rules; the App Masters now ask for accepts as reviews. Either the reviews get answered, or a policy accepts a class of items, or the loop stays bounded.
  • The unattended review policy is broader than its note says. With autonomous mode on it approves every pending review older than an hour of any severity except high or critical, ten per tick, labelled "low/medium" whatever they are. Asks are now exempt; the rest is as it was.
  • One idea was accepted by that policy, not by you. e38c24b7, the ATS webhook status item, already delivered on a branch. Left as it is and named here.
  • No pull request yet. Rung-2 shipping merged at 09:0x; no delivery has run under it since. The first PR from an App Master worker is the next thing to watch for.
  • Census is red on master for three rules from sibling sessions' files, not from this arc: machine-specific-path-in-tooling (C:/Users/kazda/… literals in scripts/templates/_migration/*.mjs and studio scripts), unobservable-detached-task, gate-without-empty-input-guard. Named, not edited behind their backs.
  • Merging restarts the dev app and kills running workers. The sweeps recover the state, at the cost of one wasted run per active persona. A production build would not have this problem; a dev loop does.
  • What is still draft. All 111 recipes. Promotion out of draft, and the registry migration, are yours; the corpus has now been used for a day rather than reviewed for an afternoon.
