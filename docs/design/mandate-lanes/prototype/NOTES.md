# Mandate Lanes

## Philosophy
A mandate is a signed goal with a checked "met when", an envelope (spend, merge policy, what the steward decides alone) and a steward. Inside the envelope the steward decides and logs; only crossings reach you. The seven chips plus `ready` become four lanes (**Breach** with valves, **Sign**, **Accept**, **Talk**) plus Orphans. Autopilot becomes **Stewards**.

## How it stays readable at scale
Today's 295 pending become 29 cards; at scale, 2,950 become 312. Breaches coalesce per mandate and clause, ranked by consequence. Levels: lane → peek → card → reader, and register (`M`) → mandate page → act log. `/` finds anything.

## The wow moment
Press `1`, then `Enter`: before any paragraph, the card says the kind, the ask and both outcomes, with its verdict keys where every card keeps them. Press `P`: arrivals land in the tray, never under the card in hand.

## Known limits
- Mandates, envelopes, acts, spend, sign forecasts, fix plans and council members are derived in code and labelled as design. Agreement rests on the invented `agentWouldHave`.
- At scale the datasets name different projects, so nameplates match mandates only for `today`.

## How this answers the owner's note
No cap, no sittings, no calendar lapse: cards arrive when they happen, and an envelope asks to be re-read when its use says so.

## What changed in round 3
The first level is untouched; everything deeper is rebuilt on the app's own deck anatomy.
- **One anatomy** (head, body, rail, dock) for every card, the reader and the mandate page: "component design".
- **The head reads in order**: kind, the ask as title, then "If you yes / If you no" side by side: "their structure".
- **A fixed dock**: yes lit, no quieter, the key inside the button; the deck never resizes, so keys never move: "quickly read and react".
- **Irreversible means armed in place**: send, delete, spend and merge ask twice and list what leaves: "popus for the decision making".
- **One-key reasons**: the prompt replaces the dock; digits pick, Enter skips, send-back needs 8 characters: "decision making".
- **After a verdict**: stamp, receipt line, next card; an empty lane offers the lanes still holding cards: "react".
- **A real reader**: progress, contents, its own rail; council verdicts show member meters and coverage: "the deeper I click".
- **Specific shapes**: incident sheet, envelope diff, met-when checklist, live diff and forecast while signing: "structure".
- **Drawer pages**: the mandate page is a card (act log first, envelope rail); counted filters; archiving orphans asks twice: "careless design".
- **Find and help**: grouped results, marked matches, starter hints; keys grouped by surface: "visual quality".
