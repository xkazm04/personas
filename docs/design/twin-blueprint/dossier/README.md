# Twin blueprint, variant `dossier`: instrument dossier

Metaphor: the twin as an instrument dossier, a bento of four kit tiles (Identity 4 + Voice 8 over Knowledge 4 + Training 8 columns). Each tile draws its section with shared instruments on declared domains: unit strips on a 1-2-5 quantum, pip meters against the slots the prompt compiler reads (5 exemplars, 8 rules), topic bars on twice the covered threshold, a style fingerprint on the 1..5 range, and a headline gauge from `sectionCoverage`. Unmeasured numbers are hatched or "-".

- `detail-*`: L1 on the one-channel twin: bio against its target, languages, readiness chips, the one voiced channel, the memory strip and ledger, six topic bars, goal columns and the kind mix.
- `detail-empty-*`: a twin that was just forged. Bio and style are hatched, Training has no plan yet, so its gauge reads "-".
- `detail-rich-*`: the overflow test. At 1280 the nine channels fold to four rows and a "+5" row of origin dots. At 1920 (the roomy form) all nine are drawn, with larger instruments.
- `detail-focus-*`: L2 for Voice. The tile grows into a full-width spec matrix: channels as columns, the eight dimensions as pips, then samples, rules and directives, with an origin legend. The other three sections sit in a rail of gauges on top. At 1280 the nine channel heads stand on two alternating tiers.
- `stage-*`: the training base layer mid-beat with the reconciled delta. The tiles stand in two side rails around an empty centre. The Training tile is lit, the goal meter shows the gain segment, and the readout gives "Coverage +12%" and the why. At 1920 the rails have room for labelled topic bars.
- `stage-working-*`: the empty twin while the engine works. The gauges carry a shimmer sweep and the centre a calm ghost under "Reading your answers" (CSS loops, frozen in a still).
- Sizes are 1280x800 and 1920x1080, in dark-midnight and light. Source: `node scripts/style/shoot.mjs --module twin/blueprint/<state> --tape synthetic --kit dossier`.
