# Twin blueprint, variant `radial`: radial anatomy

Metaphor: the twin is drawn as an anatomy plate. Its initial sits at the centre, ringed by readiness. The four sections are quadrants of a coverage ring, and each arc fills to that section's `sectionCoverage`; an arc with nothing measured is hatched and dashed. The band outside each quadrant draws that section's sub-quantities: Voice channels as spokes (samples, 0 to 5) with each channel's 8-dimension style star at the tip, Training topics as six cells (approved solid, awaiting review hatched, 0 to 5), Knowledge as an approved / awaiting / rejected tri-arc with fact ticks, and Identity as the bio arc against its target with one mark per language. Chevrons mean a count runs past its declared domain. Hairline circles, quadrant separators and a degree scale are the construction lines. Every tint comes from `--primary`.

- `detail-*`: L1 on the one-channel twin. Each quadrant carries a full-size label in its corner: the name, the coverage, and two counts.
- `detail-empty-*`: a twin that was just forged. The bio and Training (no plan yet) are hatched as not measured. Measured zeros show as empty tracks with the pen at the start.
- `detail-rich-*`: the overflow test, with nine channels, eight goals and counts past their domains. The ring takes the largest radius that still leaves its labels full-size room.
- `detail-focus-*`: L2 for Voice. The ring zoomed into the quadrant (a CSS transform; the stills show where it lands) and unfolded it into a full sub-ring. Inner cells show each channel's share of the headline; outer spokes show samples and rules side by side, each against a dashed "full" circle, with the stars on one ring. The panel holds a key and one row per channel; each row opens L3.
- `stage-*`: the training base layer, mid-beat with the reconciled delta. The anatomy sits in the rail left of the question card. Training is lit and the other three quadrants recede. The answered topic's cell lights a tick, the goal's arc grows by the gain in the gap outside the ring, and the readout gives the gain and the model's reason.
- `stage-working-*`: the empty twin while the engine works. A slow tick orbits the ring (a CSS loop) and the readout says "Reading your answers".
- Sizes are 1280x800 and 1920x1080, in the dark-midnight and light themes. Each shot comes from `node scripts/style/shoot.mjs --module twin/blueprint/<state> --tape synthetic --kit radial`. At 1280x800 the stage rail is about 184 px wide, so the figure there uses a compact tier with no chevrons, scale or star frames.
