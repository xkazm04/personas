The bench

Every key

The sky is a field of clusters: children sit on a sunflower spiral, rank 1 at the centre, alphabetical outward, and the rank is printed in the docked list too. The bench rises over the bottom of the field and never hides it; the queue orders itself by what is yours to decide, and the round table seats the five council members around one rose.
