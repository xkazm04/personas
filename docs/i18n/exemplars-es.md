# Gold exemplars — es (personas)

Register by demonstration: the locale's best reviewed pairs, one per string class where possible.
Seeded 2026-08-24 from the onboarding review wave (registry localization bundle anchors). Grow toward ~8 pairs;
replace a pair only when a better one ships.

## `onboarding.interface_mode_description`

- **en**: You can change this later in Settings.
- **es**: Puede cambiarlo más adelante en Ajustes.
- **why**: Correct usted conjugation, pro-drop, no inserted pronoun — a clean short instructional sentence.

## `onboarding.desktop_scan_error`

- **en**: Could not scan your desktop.
- **es**: No se pudo escanear su escritorio.
- **why**: Se-passive per ES-SE-PASSIVE plus correct usted possessive — idiomatic error copy, not a calque.

## `onboarding.tour_explore_to_continue`

- **en**: Take your time. Click below when you've explored this section.
- **es**: Tómese su tiempo. Haga clic abajo cuando haya explorado esta sección.
- **why**: Two correctly conjugated usted imperatives (Tómese, Haga) in a row — the exact conversion the house style guide's table asks for.

## `onboarding.because_you_connected`

- **en**: Because you connected {apps}, these templates fit best:
- **es**: Debido a que conectó {apps}, estas son las plantillas que mejor encajan:
- **why**: Correct third-person usted preterite around an interpolated placeholder, with the placeholder left untouched.

## `onboarding.tour_storage_unavailable_toast`

- **en**: Your browser blocked our local storage, so the tour will restart each session. Try clearing site data or leaving private browsing.
- **es**: Su navegador bloqueó nuestro almacenamiento local, por lo que el recorrido se reiniciará en cada sesión. Intente borrar los datos del sitio o salir del modo de navegación privada.
- **why**: A long toast that stays usted throughout (Su, Intente) without resorting to a stilted inserted pronoun — good length control on a wordy source.
