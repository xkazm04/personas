# The Loudest Reason

## The one idea

Nine reasons score a subject; the row prints the one that decides it, in the scan's own
sentence, plus one measure. The measure is **drawn so it admits what it left out**: the
strip is the dominant reason's points in its tone, then the rest as hollow units. A subject
whose one sentence covers all its weight and one whose sentence covers a third look
different at rest. That hollow remainder invites the descent, and the descent is a **split,
not a swap** — the row stays put, the same strip reappears below at the same scale with the
remainder resolved into real channels, and the nine list in the registry's own dominance
order, so the sentence you already read is line one, tagged "on the row". Leaving eight out
becomes a summary you can audit, not a loss.

## How the two columns divide

Left: rank, subject, one sentence, one measure. Right: the lane as a working surface —
search (`/`), a state filter, one chip isolating what the pre-processing pass has not read.
Its head draws the sitting as units split by what the pass found.

Four inks span **both** halves: a count, a measured zero (ring + "none"), an unknown
(dashed box), an unmeasurable (hatched box) — shape before colour. The plan runs through the
app's own `buildModel`, so those four are the app's judgement, not this variant's.

## What a queued intake looks like before enrichment

The URL he pasted, in code type, with `intake · [not read yet]` under it. Forty stack their
dashed pills into one visible column. A resource the pass read and could not name is a
third thing: `[nothing to name]`, hatched, with its reason verbatim.
