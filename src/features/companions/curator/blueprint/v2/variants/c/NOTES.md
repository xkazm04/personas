# The Filing Bench

## The one idea

**The bundle is the join.** The pre-read pass returns a topic *and a high-level
domain*, and that domain is the vocabulary her plan is indexed by. So the last
cell of every plan row is *how many of his intakes point at this bundle*, and
pressing it filters his queue to them.

That cell is where the page's law starts working rather than being carried. The
figure is what was **measured**; what sits outside it is drawn beside it in its
own ink - grey for intakes nobody has read yet (**unknown**, not zero), amber
for resources that could never be read (**unmeasurable**). A clean `0` gets the
hollow measured-zero mark. Four facts, four Tone x Glyph pairs, four words, one
file (`Ink.tsx`) both columns draw through.

## How the two columns divide

Left is his, designed first: paste box, state lens, forty rows. Right is hers,
compressed - the nine channels became one drawn quantity (`UnitStrip`: a unit =
6 points, coloured by weight band, shaped by reason), the reason still named in
words. Her opening sentence is addressed to him. **"Needs you"** crosses both
honesty axes - a failed *request* and an unreadable *resource* sit together,
because at forty-at-a-time he does not care which broke.

## What a queued intake looks like before enrichment

The link he pasted, in `typo-code`, elided - an ordered, identified row with
"not read yet" beside it in the unknown ink. Not a shimmer: it is filed, only
its topic is missing. A failed read keeps that link and offers **re-read**.

## Known limits

Fixture-backed; both tables are empty today, and a link outside it stays
unknown. `useBench` schedules the deliveries `COMPANIONS_STATUS_CHANGED` will
carry. No keyboard cursor over the rows.
