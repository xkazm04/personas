# Template decision table (131 templates)

Fill the **Decision** column. Suggested vocabulary:

- `recipe` — the whole thing is really one capability; it becomes a recipe (a responsibility seed) and stops being a preset agent.

- `agent` — a genuine preset agent; keep it, adjusted to the manifest + charters schema.

- `merge:<name>` — fold into another template.

- `drop` — retire it.


Columns: **UC** = use cases it declares, **Ref** = how many of those are backed by a recipe.


> The 8 rows with `UC 0` declare no capability at all. They are the clearest `recipe`/`drop` candidates,
> and worth deciding first because they cannot become agents as they stand.
