## 12:58 - PATTERN - Where a recipe already says "the human owns the verdict", the correction is never the verdict

- Every contract recipe in this lane carries an explicit boundary sentence: the review
  prepares a reviewer's judgment and the person who signs owns it. So a single overrule is
  the recipe succeeding, and writing a correction loop around it would contradict the
  recipe's own best line.
- The enrichment that survives is one level up. Not the overruled finding but the
  **repetition** of the same overrule across items, which is evidence about the stored
  thing the finding was generated from: the playbook position, the routing rule, the lead
  time estimate, the prior about what stalls a counterparty.
- Phrasing that carried it without bloating, and it is worth stealing: "one reviewer
  overruling one finding is the review working as designed, and the same overrule
  repeating is evidence the preferred position is no longer one this team will hold out
  for." The concession is what makes the claim land.

## 13:00 - TRAP - In compliance work, an accepted correction must stay VISIBLE, not be suppressed

- Everywhere else in this pass the payoff of a correction is that the next run stops
  raising the thing. In an audited account that is the wrong move: a finding that was
  raised, reviewed and accepted has to remain readable as an accepted exception, or the
  account now shows a control that never fired.
- So part 3 for `access-grant-audit-and-compliance-reporting` is a split: stop presenting
  it as a fresh question, keep it in the account as answered. Check for this anywhere the
  output is evidence for somebody outside the team.

## 13:02 - NO - Two nos, and the second one is the useful one

- `contract-envelope-event-sync`: no. It reads envelopes back, refuses out of order writes
  and collapses repeats. Its only person-facing call is which status kinds interrupt
  somebody, and that is a configured list in `personalization_needs`, not a per item
  judgment. A stall flag disagreement is a threshold correction. Nothing to overrule.
- `contract-renewal-window-watch`: no, and this one took real deliberation. It sits beside
  `contract-obligation-and-deadline-watch`, which I did enrich, and the two share a
  decision lead time knob. The cut I made: obligation watch's whole thesis is that the
  lead time is heterogeneous per obligation (a signed certificate versus an email) and is
  the recipe's own estimate, so only the owner's experience calibrates it. Renewal watch's
  thesis is the arithmetic, and its distinguishing inputs (the notice period, the early
  edge of the window) are terms of the contract, where a disagreement corrects the
  **register** and not the recipe.
- The generalisable test: if the disputed number could be settled by rereading the source
  document, it is a data defect and not a correction. If it could only be settled by
  somebody who did the work telling you how long it took, it is a correction.
- Sharper reason to say no here than either of those: had I enriched both, the two
  sentences would have been swappable, which is the brief's own failure condition. Where
  two recipes in one lane share a dial, only the one whose thesis IS that dial should get
  the enrichment.

## 13:04 - PATTERN - Reader silence is already counted; the reason for it is not

- `product-idea-harvesting-from-codebase-and-web` already tracks acceptance per source and
  already treats a candidate nobody touched as a rejection. That looked like a closed loop
  (cp-01 and cp-02 both flag this class as a probable no).
- It is not closed, because the loop mis-attributes. The recipe's own named worst failure
  is the candidate the product already shipped, and that rejection is evidence the
  grounding read of the codebase missed a feature, not evidence the source is bad. Docking
  the source sends the harvest to mine elsewhere while the blind spot stays exactly where
  it was.
- Worth checking wherever an acceptance rate already exists: is every rejection reason
  being charged to the same account? If two different failures are pooled into one score,
  the loop is measuring but not learning.
