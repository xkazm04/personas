## 10:26 - NO - Pure dispatch recipes: the judgment belongs to the recipe upstream

- `approved-draft-channel-publishing` was my only no of nine. It carries an approval
  through to a channel and reports what happened. Everything it emits is a fact about the
  world (live, queued, handed back, failed) and facts are wrong, not overruled.
- The trap: it LOOKS rich in correction signal because the word approval is all over it.
  But the approval is its input, not its output. Whoever drafted the thing owns the taste.
- Generalizable test for the other lanes: if you can only correct the recipe by fixing a
  CONFIGURATION VALUE it was given (a channel limit, a duplicate rule, a threshold), that
  is a repair, not a correction. A correction overrules a call the recipe made itself.
- Look for this in anything shaped as deliver, dispatch, file, route, sync, notify.

## 10:26 - PATTERN - An approval with the artifact edited is the richest signal and every pipeline discards it

- Several of mine already recorded REJECTIONS with reasons and recorded nothing about
  approvals. But a reviewer who rewrites two lines and then approves has handed you the
  correction with the corrected artifact attached, which is strictly more information than
  a rejection reason someone had to articulate.
- Phrasing that carried all three parts in one criterion without bloat: name the edited
  artifact, say it is recorded as a correction rather than as a yes, keep the before beside
  the after, then one because-clause saying where the signal dies today.
- If a recipe already has a rejection ledger, that ledger is the home. You do not need to
  invent a durable record, and saying so keeps the criterion to one sentence.

## 10:26 - PATTERN - Route the correction to the thing that was actually wrong

- The best two in my lane do not say "record the correction". They say what the correction
  is EVIDENCE OF, which is a different and sharper claim:
  - a rejection that passed every check is a finding about the CHECK LIST, not the asset;
  - a reject the recipe attributed to stage A that the human sends to stage B is a
    correction of the ATTRIBUTION, not of the draft.
- This is also what saved me from a template. Eight recipes all had a human overruling
  something, and if I had written eight sentences about recording that, they would have
  been swappable. Naming the specific durable thing that must change made every sentence
  unusable in any other recipe.

## 10:26 - DECISION - Guidance was the wrong home in all eight, on purpose

- My lane ran 77 to 88 words of guidance, six of nine at 81 or more. I evicted nothing.
- In each case the guidance was already carrying the craft claim that makes the recipe
  worth reading, and the correction claim is about what the recipe KEEPS between runs,
  which is what success criteria under a durable-record outcome are for. Several of these
  recipes already had an outcome shaped exactly like that (settings carried forward, held
  for a decision, kept work is traceable) and the criterion landed there with no strain.
- Recommend the same default: if the recipe has an outcome about what survives to the next
  run, that outcome is the home and guidance never comes into it.
