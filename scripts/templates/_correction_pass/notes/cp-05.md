## 10:26 - PATTERN - Suppression is what makes a threshold correctable

- Two recipes in this lane both flag against a rolling baseline, and only one earned an
  enrichment. The discriminator that held: does the recipe **withhold** anything from the
  person, or does it show them everything and highlight some of it?
- `high-intent-lead-alerting` and `content-metrics-anomaly-check` suppress. Below the
  threshold, nothing reaches a person at all, so "you should not have woken me" and "I had
  to find that myself" are both real corrections with real consequences.
- `paid-organic-performance-scan` flags inside a report the reader has already opened.
  Nothing is hidden. A disagreement with a flag there is a disagreement with arithmetic
  against a baseline, not with a judgment. It got a `no`.
- If your recipe flags but never suppresses, check whether there is anything a human could
  actually be denied by the call before you enrich it.

## 10:26 - NO - A recipe that has deliberately drawn a line at observation

- `paid-organic-performance-scan` says in its own outcome: "The read stops at observation.
  The changes it argues for belong to whoever proposes them." That line is craft, and it is
  the reason the recipe answers no.
- The overrulable call in that family of work is the attribution or the proposal, and this
  recipe deliberately does not make either. Adding a correction loop would have dragged it
  back across the boundary it drew on purpose, which is a worse outcome than the gap.
- Look for this sentence shape in your own lane. When a recipe has explicitly handed the
  judgment to a downstream recipe, the correction belongs downstream too.

## 10:26 - PATTERN - The strongest enrichment is often "the record does not exist yet"

- `lead-welcome-email-drafting` was the sharpest case in this lane. A human edits the draft
  before sending, so the correction arrives with the corrected artifact attached, which is
  richer than any verdict.
- But the recipe records only **what was sent**. The draft is overwritten by it, so the diff
  (the whole signal) is unrecoverable by construction. Naming that, per the brief's point 2,
  was the enrichment: keep the draft beside what went out.
- Generalisable: wherever a recipe records a final artifact and a human touches it before it
  becomes final, check whether the pre-human version survives. Usually it does not, and
  saying so is more valuable than describing a loop that has nowhere to land.
