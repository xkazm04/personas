## 13:20 - DECISION - Two recipes on the same billing data, and only one has a call to overrule

- The three cloud recipes in this lane share a data source and split cleanly, which is the
  useful part. `cloud-cost-and-resource-reporting` is my clearest no of the two. Everything
  a reader would dispute in it turns out to be a dial they already own: what counts as a
  move worth naming is the `sensitivity` enum, attribution comes from the labels on the
  bill rather than from a judgment, the unattributable share is measured, and its knowledge
  of which changes were deliberate is a declared `personalization_need` that the adopter
  supplies rather than something the recipe infers. Correcting any of that is cp-06's
  repair, not a correction.
- `cloud-spend-threshold-watch` reads almost identically and is a yes, on one clause: it
  PROJECTS. A projection is a claim about a period that has not happened, and "that was the
  annual invoice, not a trajectory" overrules the claim rather than adjusting a knob. The
  recipe already makes that call itself in a criterion.
- Discriminator to carry: a report of a closed period has nothing to be wrong about yet. A
  watch that extrapolates does. Where two recipes in your lane read the same source, check
  which of them commits to a statement the data has not yet settled.

## 13:24 - NO - An existing loop counts only if it names a HOME, not just an intent

- Both investing recipes already mentioned reader feedback and they landed on opposite
  sides. `under-covered-equity-discovery-by-sector` says it in four places: a `retune`
  activity, two criteria, and the `input` description reading "the thresholds as the
  reader's earlier verdicts have moved them". The home is the thresholds and it is named
  everywhere. That is a no, and a fifth mention would be the padding the brief warns about.
- `congressional-stock-disclosure-coverage` had exactly one line, "usefulness feedback from
  the reader tightens the next pass's filters rather than being logged and ignored". Every
  word of the intent is there and not one of the three parts is: no object corrected, no
  home, no mechanism. It is the sentence you would write if you were applying a template.
- So the count of mentions is not the test. Ask whether the recipe names the durable thing
  the verdict is filed against. If it does not, the enrichment is a REWRITE of that line
  and not an addition; a second feedback criterion beside a vague one is two sentences
  where the corpus needs one.

## 13:28 - PATTERN - Where a misclassification corrupts a COUNT, that is the whole enrichment

- `cloud-billing-access-failure-alerting` keys its recurrence record by failure mode, and
  its best outcome is that a recurring mode becomes a standing fix instead of news. That
  makes the classification load bearing in a way a verdict usually is not: a wrong mode
  does not just mislabel one alert, it splits one standing problem into two rare ones and
  each of them keeps qualifying as news forever.
- The enrichment wrote itself once that was seen, and it needed no invented ledger. Worth
  checking anywhere a recipe aggregates its own judgments: if the judgment is the KEY that
  the durable record is grouped by, the correction is not about the item at all, it is
  about the integrity of every count built on top of it.
- Same shape, different lane, as cp-06's "route the correction to the thing that was
  actually wrong".

## 13:31 - TRAP - The lane hint's boundary is real and it is a tail clause, not a whole sentence

- Two investing recipes here surface rather than advise on purpose, and both already say so
  in an outcome. The risk in enriching them is that a correction loop quietly converts a
  reader's verdict into a claim about the market, which is the one thing they are written
  to refuse.
- It did not need a new criterion or a caveat paragraph. In `watchlist-technical-signal-digest`
  the guard is the back half of the same sentence: the reviewer's declines move what the
  digest STATES AS A CALL, and never stand as evidence the method was right or wrong about
  the market, which only the recorded outcome can answer. One clause, and it also explains
  why the loop is safe.
- Generalisable wherever a recipe holds a no-advice or no-recommendation line: write the
  correction's SCOPE into the criterion instead of trusting the existing boundary outcome
  to hold it. The loop is new and the boundary was written before it existed.
