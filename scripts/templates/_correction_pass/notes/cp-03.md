## 11:34 - PATTERN - The measure-versus-judge split does not fall where the recipe's title suggests

- My lane was flagged as monitoring work that "measures rather than judges". Six of the
  eleven turned out to judge hard: they rank by consequence, damp what triage called noise,
  decide who gets woken. The measuring is the input, not the output.
- Useful discriminator: read the `decide` activities, not the title. A recipe with two or
  more `kind: "decide"` activities is almost always a yes. A recipe whose only decide step
  is "is this above the threshold" is the real no.

## 11:36 - PATTERN - Where the correction lands is the field the recipe already argues about

- Every yes in this lane landed the correction in machinery the recipe had already named and
  already defended: the held out set, the ranking, the baseline bucket, the invariant, the
  correlation window, the release threshold, the public surface declaration, the premise
  check, the carried inventory state.
- That is also the anti-template test, for free. If your sentence would still parse with the
  destination swapped out for another recipe's, you have not found the recipe's own
  machinery, you have written "record it somewhere".

## 11:38 - TRAP - Two adjacent recipes in one lane will produce the same sentence unless you separate them by mechanism

- database-health-check and database-performance-baseline-monitoring both end in "the
  operator says this is noise". Written naively they were interchangeable.
- Separating them by the machinery the correction touches fixed it: health-check corrects an
  ORDERING (a finding moves down the ranked list); performance-baseline corrects a DAMPING
  and needs it to stay REVERSIBLE, because that one is keyed to hour and weekday buckets and
  a dismissal a month ago must not silence the same pattern once it turns real.
- Same lane, adjacent subject, and the two sentences no longer fit each other's recipe. If
  you have two recipes in the same corner, write them in the same sitting so you can see it.

## 11:40 - NO - The refusal-loop recipes are done, and re-enriching them is the padding the brief names

- scouted-opportunity-triage already carries all three parts at full strength: a typed reason
  from a closed set, recorded against the TOPIC rather than the item, a stated choice between
  dropping and ranking down, and the change reported back so the reader sees it happen. There
  is nothing to add that is not outcome 3 in other words.
- infrastructure-metrics-scan carries it too, spread across input ("what earlier triage
  decided about which deviations were noise") and a `damp` activity that records the damping.
  Its guidance is at the 90 word ceiling, and the only criterion I could have added was
  reversibility, which is word for word what database-performance-baseline-monitoring needed.
  Writing both would have been the swap-test failure with my own name on it.
- Class rule for the other nine: a triage or intake gate whose PRIMARY output is a decline
  usually has the loop already, because the decline is the product. Look instead at the call
  that gets no evidence, per cp-01's 11:06.

## 11:42 - PATTERN - The false hold and the false decline are where the yes hides in a "complete" recipe

- Following cp-01's 11:06 I reversed two of my own no calls, and both were the same shape:
  the recipe records what it PASSED and never learns about what it HELD.
- release-candidate-review records a decline in full, but the increment it quietly judged not
  worth releasing yet generates no evidence at all: from inside the work, a hold nobody minded
  and a hold that was wrong look identical. The correction belongs against the THRESHOLD.
- harvested-idea-triage re-admits a stale idea when the code moves again. If the premise check
  was simply wrong the code never moves, so that idea is the one thing in the recipe that can
  never come back on its own. The correction belongs against the PREMISE CHECK, not the idea.
- Ask of any recipe that looks finished: what does this suppress, and what would ever tell it
  the suppression was wrong.

## 11:44 - DECISION - Ground truth arriving late is a stronger correction signal than a human opinion, and one recipe in this lane has it free

- deploy-regression-correlation ranks hypotheses about which change caused an error jump, and
  the real cause is established later by the incident itself, at no extra cost.
- Its window is currently "earned" from the shipping rate, which is a prior. Resolved incidents
  are the posterior and nobody was writing them back. If a recipe in your lane produces a
  prediction that reality later settles, that is the correction to wire, ahead of any opinion.
