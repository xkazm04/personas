## 11:05 - PATTERN - The three parts fit in one criterion if you name a noun only this recipe owns

- The sentence that carries all three without bloating has a fixed skeleton but a
  recipe-specific subject: `<the disputed call>, is recorded against <the durable
  thing that already exists here>, so <the named dial in this recipe> moves`.
- The swap test passes or fails on the middle clause. If you write "recorded in the
  ledger" you have written a template. If you write "recorded against the provider
  reason that produced that judgment", it cannot be lifted into another recipe.
- Look in `personalization_needs` first. Half of mine already declared the exact
  unknown the correction tunes ("which causes here resolve without anybody", "how
  long after a closure a return still counts as the same issue", "which states
  legitimately stop that clock"). A correction that lands on a declared unknown
  writes itself: the setup answer stops being a setup answer.

## 11:08 - TRAP - Two of six ended "rather than ... at setup" and I nearly shipped it

- The side-by-side check at the end of the brief is not ceremonial. My six were all
  distinct in subject and still two had converged on the same closing cadence.
- Cadence is how a template hides from a swap test: the nouns differ so it reads
  unique, but the shape is identical and the second one adds nothing the first did
  not already teach the reader. Vary the tail clause, not just the subject.
- Cheap check: print your lane's added sentences alone, with the recipe names
  stripped, and read only the last eight words of each.

## 11:12 - PATTERN - Look for a learning loop that already exists and is aimed at only one judgment

- `issue-source-coverage-and-triage` already had a full correction loop ("a rejected
  solution loses confidence so it stops being suggested") - but scoped to one of its
  three judgments. Its triage call (real work vs close) and its escalate call had no
  loop at all.
- That is a better finding than a bare gap, and it makes the enrichment un-swappable
  for free, because you can write "moves the bar the same way a rejected solution
  moves confidence" and reference the machinery by name.
- So: before deciding a recipe is already covered, check WHICH judgment the existing
  loop covers. Partial coverage looked like full coverage in two of my six on first
  read.

## 11:15 - DECISION - Six yes, zero no, and why that is lane-specific not a failure of rigour

- The brief expects a real number of NOs and I have none. Customer support is the
  degenerate case: every recipe here classifies, disposes or escalates, and all six
  recur. The hint in my assignment said as much.
- The nearest miss was `issue-handling-trend-review`, which mostly measures and
  reports. It survived on one clause: it does not just report that speed rose and
  reopens rose, it claims that is ONE finding rather than two. That interpretive
  claim is the overrulable thing, not the figures.
- Useful discriminator for other lanes: a recipe that only reports numbers still
  qualifies if it asserts a RELATION between them that a reader can dispute. A
  recipe that reports numbers side by side and lets the reader draw the line does
  not.

## 11:18 - PATTERN - Guidance eviction is cheapest where guidance restates a criterion verbatim

- Only one of my six went into guidance (74w, the most room in the lane) and it
  still needed an eviction to stay under 90.
- What came out was "A first account is a baseline, not a change." - which the recipe
  already said, in more detail, as a success criterion under its archive outcome.
  That is the brief's eviction test met exactly, and it cost the recipe nothing.
- Guidance that ends with a short aphoristic sentence is worth checking first: in a
  corpus rendered from outcomes, a closing aphorism is usually a compression of a
  criterion sitting fifteen lines above it.

## 11:40 - TRAP - cp-06's repair test caught one of mine after I had already written it

- cp-06's rule (if the only fix is a CONFIGURATION VALUE the recipe was given, that is a
  repair not a correction) applies hardest to recipes whose disputed thing is also a
  declared `personalization_need`. Mine was the SLA clock: "which states legitimately stop
  that clock here" is a setup answer, so a criterion that amends that list is editing a
  knob, however correction-shaped the sentence reads.
- It survived, but only after rewording. The saving fact was that the recipe already makes
  its own call in the same area ("a request that has waited on the customer for longer than
  the promise itself is surfaced rather than left parked"), so the correction could be
  routed into what the work SURFACES rather than into the configured list.
- General form: when the correction lands on a personalization need, do not have it rewrite
  the answer. Have it change what the recipe does with requests that answer says to ignore.
  Same evidence, and it stays a correction rather than becoming a settings change.

## 11:44 - PATTERN - The closest pair in a lane is the real swap test, and it is usually two recipes over the same underlying event

- cp-09's rule (where two recipes share a dial, only the one whose thesis IS that dial gets
  it) generalises further: my two nearest were both about reopened tickets, from opposite
  sides. `support-issue-disposition-account` asks whether one resolution held;
  `issue-handling-trend-review` asks whether a rise in returned work is real or an artifact.
- Same underlying event, and the enrichments still cannot be swapped, because each names a
  mechanism the other does not have: a return window, and an archived comparison basis.
- So the discriminator is not "do these two recipes look at the same thing" but "does each
  one own a different mechanism the correction can move". If they look at the same event and
  own the same mechanism, cp-09 is right and only one should get it.

## 11:46 - DECISION - Support recipes carry a correction the other lanes may not have: the customer's own dispute

- Five of my six can be corrected by a colleague. One can be corrected by the person the
  work was done for, which is a different and better signal, because it arrives unprompted
  and about the cases the team was most confident on.
- Worth looking for in any lane whose output reaches somebody outside the team: a reopen, a
  reply saying this did not answer it, a complaint that arrives while the number is green.
  cp-08's suppression discriminator misses these, since nothing was suppressed; the claim
  was simply wrong and the person on the other end is the only one who knows.
