## 14:05 - TRAP - The most common reason was not on the list, and it is that "correct" is an adjective

- The brief gave three reasons the correction vocabulary could be present. Measured across
  the 21, the most common is a fourth one nobody had: **the matched token is the adjective**.
  The whole of the evidence in ten of them reads "several hundred correct findings",
  "a correct total from a plausible one", "defending the name may still be correct", "often

  "whether or not it was correct", "thirty correct drafts", "look correct in hindsight". Not
  one of those is a correction of anything the recipe judged. Seven recipes have nothing else.
- Two more were withheld on a homonym in a different sense entirely: in
  `revenue-movement-report-and-prediction-calibration` a "correction" is a **weight
  adjustment** ("so a later reader can tell a correction from a drift"), and in
  `weighted-decision-analysis-and-checkpoint-review` "override" is what the requester does to
  the matrix, which is the exact gap the pass exists to close, cited as a failure.
- `work-candidate-triage-gate` contains **zero** matches for correct / overrule / overturn /
  override in any inflection, so whatever excluded it was a broader refusal vocabulary
  (refuse, decline, reject, reverse) and that widens the false-exclusion set well past the 21.
- The honest conclusion for the director's question: this class of filter is **not**
  recoverable by refining the vocabulary. A part-of-speech-aware version would still have
  withheld `memory-and-knowledge-base-health-audit` and `skill-library-audit`, which are the
  two the finding was actually about. The only filter that works asks whose judgment is
  being overruled, which is the question itself and not a proxy for it.

## 14:10 - PATTERN - Reason 2 has a signature and you can find it before reading the outcomes

- In all five, the correction appears in the **input or the ranking**, never in an outcome
  about what survives to the next run. `memory-and-knowledge-base-health-audit` ranks a
  recorded correction above every other finding; `skill-library-audit` reads corrections out
  of the usage record as its sharpest drift evidence; `data-assistant-quality-review` mines
  answers users corrected. In each, the recipe is a **consumer of somebody else's correction
  loop** and has none of its own.
- That is also where the best enrichment sits, because a mined correction carries an
  assumption the recipe never checks. The health audit ranks correction-derived findings top
  on the claim that the named content is what produced the wrong answer, which
  `correction-root-cause-and-propagation` (same lane) exists to say is often false. So the
  enrichment is not "learn from corrections", which the recipe visibly already does. It is
  that the reviewer who reads that content and finds it fine has falsified the recipe's own
  top-ranked class, and today that lands as one more tick in the acted-on share.
- General form: where a recipe consumes a correction as evidence, ask what it INFERRED from
  it, and enrich the inference rather than the loop.

## 14:18 - PATTERN - The class that is reliably a no here is the one whose PRODUCT is a written standard

- `product-design-brief-authoring` and `per-channel-voice-profiling` are the same recipe in
  two domains: derive a written standard from real work, specific enough to reject something.
  Both already carry all three parts, because a standard nobody revises from corrections has
  no reason to exist. The brief says it outright ("Work rejected repeatedly on the same brief
  dimension is read as the brief being wrong, not as three separate failures of the work").
- I still split them, and the reason is worth carrying. The brief recipe has a running
  outcome (`drift-is-visible`) that names which side is being corrected, product or brief.
  The voice recipe tests accept-and-reject **once, at authoring**, and every running signal it
  takes afterwards is a correction on a draft that got through. cp-08's `sanitized-corpus-derivation`
  distinction exactly: thinking about ambiguity at derivation time is not a loop.
- So the voice recipe's enrichment is the never-say list. A wrongly banned line produces no
  draft, therefore no correction, therefore nothing the clustering counter can ever see: the
  strictest part of the description is the one part nothing can contradict. That is the
  suppressed half in a recipe that has no queue and no cap, which is where to look once the
  held-back-item sentence is taken.

## 14:22 - DECISION - skill-library-audit judged, not edited, and the sentence is about the DECLINE it already records

- Confirmed reason 2: "a correction right after somebody reached for an entry marks the moment
  it was believed and was wrong" is a drift signal read out of somebody else's usage record.
  Nothing in the recipe learns from being wrong, so it was a false exclusion.
- The obvious targets are both dead ends. The disuse enquiry is already covered ("a proposal
  the person has already declined is not offered again within the cooldown, and the reason it
  was declined is carried with the entry") and the held-back-by-the-cap set is the shipped
  sentence from `production-error-issue-filter`.
- What is genuinely uncovered is that the recipe pools two unlike declines under one cooldown.
  Told "the entry was right all along", the maintainer has **confirmed** it, and this recipe's
  own strongest instrument is a last-confirmed date kept deliberately apart from a last-edited
  date. That date never advances, so after the cooldown the audit reads the entry as still
  unconfirmed and proposes the same drift again. Sentence I would have put under
  `the-library-can-still-be-trusted`:

  > A maintainer who declines an edit by saying the entry was right all along has confirmed
  > it, so that decline advances the entry's last confirmed date and is kept apart from a
  > decline about how the proposal was shaped, because pooling the two damps the entry under
  > one cooldown while the audit goes on reading it as unconfirmed and proposes the same
  > drift again the moment that cooldown expires.

  File untouched, per the assignment.
