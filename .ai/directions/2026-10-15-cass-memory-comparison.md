## 0. Premises, and what the tree corrected

The director's four premises hold at `ac0b6bc105`:

1. The always-on lane orders by importance, then recency (`src-tauri/src/companion/brain/procedural.rs:232 "ORDER BY n.importance DESC, n.updated_at DESC"`). It takes six rules (`src-tauri/src/companion/brain/retrieval.rs:97 "const ALWAYS_INCLUDE_TOP_PROCEDURALS: u32 = 6;"`).
2. Every lifecycle query in consolidation is fact-only (`src-tauri/src/companion/brain/consolidation.rs:598 "WHERE n.kind = 'fact'"`). Rules never decay.
3. The harness world decides failure with no reference to any backend (`evals/memory-year/memory_year/world.py:340 "rng.random() < 0.45"`). Once the fix is taught, that task kind stops failing for good. That also happens regardless of recall.
4. Recall is a probe (`evals/memory-year/memory_year/backends/__init__.py:31 "is a probe read: no usage feedback may result"`).

The tree corrected five points in the personas read:

- **There are three writers, not two.** Staged deltas from another device go through the same apply path as compress (`src-tauri/src/companion/brain/sleep_cycle/sync_inbox.rs:18 "Drain the sync inbox through the SAME validate/apply path"`). They do so without the shown-episode check, and their provenance is the delta itself (`src-tauri/src/companion/brain/sleep_cycle/sync_inbox.rs:22 "does NOT face is provenance-against-this-machine's-episodes"`).
- **A correction rule does not wait for some later cycle.** Reconcile seeds on the rules the same cycle wrote (`src-tauri/src/companion/brain/sleep_cycle/phases.rs:229 "&notes.written_procedural_ids,"`). A new rule and its predecessor can therefore be paired the night the correction is learned, provided the triggers overlap.
- **Confidence is not just unused. On the unattended path the model's own value is discarded.** Compress writes a constant (`src-tauri/src/companion/brain/sleep_cycle/apply.rs:329 "confidence: DEFAULT_CONFIDENCE,"`). Yet the prompt still renders it to the model as a percentage (`src-tauri/src/companion/prompt/memory.rs:190 "(imp {imp}, conf {conf:.0}%, from {srcs})"`).
- **The harness cannot see the exposure hazard on `last_used_at`.** The neutral adapter's ingest never recalls, and its recall always sets the probe flag (`evals/memory-year/memory_year/backends/athena.py:93 "True})"`). So in a harness year a rule's `last_used_at` stays at its write time. In production the always-on six are restarted on every turn (`src-tauri/src/companion/brain/retrieval.rs:709 "let _ = procedural::touch_last_used(pool, &ids);"`). This matters for §6.1.
- **Peer-read anchors**: eight resolve only from a subdirectory (`world.py`, `temporal_vote.py`, `run.py` from `evals/memory-year/memory_year/`, plus bare `apply.rs`, `catalog.rs`, `procedural.rs`, `constitution.md`), and its FINDINGS:329 quote breaks on a backtick. Every anchor below uses the repo-relative path.

The always-on degeneration is already stated in the registry corpus (`recall-injection`: rank by importance, then recency, while every auto-written item shares one importance, and the tier becomes the most recent N). personas also records it itself (`evals/memory-year/FINDINGS.md:181 "every cycle-written rule shares an importance"`). So §5.2 describes a **standing deviation**, not a new finding.

## The measurement that governs the feedback verdicts

Run-scratchpad `exposure_credit.py`, deterministic, no model calls. Input: the harness's recorded per-probe contexts of the self-hosted service arm (194 probes, 59-100 items delivered each, accuracy 0.92 / 0.90 in two read modes). Every delivered item was credited with the probe's graded outcome, cm's rule, blame withheld for sets over 8.

- **Scores track exposure** (Spearman 0.82 / 0.81), not support of the gold answer (0.06 / 0.05). 25 of 36 and 23 of 33 items at cm's proven bar never supported any probe.
- **The blame guard never fired**: no set was 8 or smaller, so the rule was pure credit. Symmetric blame was worse (44 / 69 useful items below neutral).
- **Credit through use** raised top-25 precision (0.92 / 0.96 vs 0.36 / 0.32) but failed the pre-registered floor (7 / 11 useful items below neutral vs 0): blame through use lands on supersedence records that carry old and new value. A post-hoc no-blame variant held the floor, covered 56% of useful items and promoted nothing in a year.

**Caveat:** that arm stores verbatim records, not Athena's rules; it prices the attribution rule, not her store. T2 runs it on Athena.

**The asymmetry.** cm and ee hold task tips for coding agents: a tip matters when relevant, and an outcome (verification, bead close, revert) follows the work. personas holds conduct rules for one operator that must apply on turns that never mention them (`src-tauri/src/companion/brain/retrieval.rs:96 "stay in force regardless of query phrasing."`), and no downstream event says a turn went well because a rule was in force. The sources' scoring has no input here, and the cheapest input, exposure, is worse than none.

## What personas does better

- **It measures memory.** Neither source shows its rules help; personas has a year-long replay, a stale-answer count, a sign-test tie band and a one-variable fixed-store instrument.
- **Provenance is enforced in code and checked against the prompt.** cm admits a rule on regex-labelled keyword hits.
- **Rule text is immutable.** A change is a new rule plus a compare-and-set supersede; cm lets a model rewrite a rule under the same id and history.
- **Retirement exists and was priced** (0 to 68 of 131, accuracy up). cm's deprecation is absorbing; its only automatic exit is dead code.
- **No credit, no exposure loop.** Absence costs a lane with no merit key, not one with a corrupted key; on the evidence above, the better failure.
- **A probe is not a use, in both the brain and the harness.** cm had to retrofit the equivalent guard after its own calls corrupted playbooks.
